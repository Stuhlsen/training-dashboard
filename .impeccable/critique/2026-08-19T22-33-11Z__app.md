## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Strongly specific in vocabulary, generic in composition. The domain language is real: Coggan zones Z1–Z5 with a Sweet-Spot overlay, Foster Monotonie/Strain, Seiler polarization, a "Ruhetag never counts as missed" rule baked into the code, per-session-type accent bars in the week grid. No generic dashboard template could wear this unchanged. But the presentation fights it: a full-bleed stock photo of a pro peloton sits behind every page, the Hero content is a mouse-tracked 3D tilt plate, and the declared type system (Sora / IBM Plex Mono / Inter) never actually loads — every number you see is a system-font fallback. The design system exists more on paper than on screen.

**Deterministic scan:** The static CLI scan (`detect.mjs` over `app/` source) came back clean — `[]`, twice. But the *live browser* overlay (which checks actually-rendered, computed styles) found real issues on every one of the 4 pages checked:

| Route | Findings | Dominant patterns |
|---|---|---|
| `/` (Hero) | 37 | undersized-ui-text (9.9–10.9px labels), text-overflow, radial-spotlight-glow, dark-glow, hero-eyebrow-chip, kicker-above-heading, overused-font (Inter 86%) |
| `/planning` | 188 | undersized-ui-text (10.24px day labels, ×13 weeks), side-tab, line-length (~157 chars/line), ai-color-palette (×3, likely false positive — see below), overused-font (Inter 74%) |
| `/analysis` | 85 | undersized-ui-text, tiny-text, line-length (up to ~258 chars/line), overused-font (Inter 63%), em-dash-overuse (13×) |
| `/log` | 111 | undersized-ui-text (table headers + week/plan tags at 10.88px), cramped-padding, overused-font (Inter 92%) |

The "overused-font: Inter" hit on literally all 4 pages is the detector independently confirming Assessment A's font-loading finding from a completely different angle (computed styles vs. reading `index.html`) — that's the strongest cross-validated finding in this run.

One likely false positive: `ai-color-palette` on `/planning` (purple/violet text) is almost certainly the documented `--vo2` zone color (#a24ad0), an intentional, meaningful part of the zone system per AGENTS.md — not AI-generated slop. Treat it as a non-issue.

## Overall Impression

The coaching logic underneath is genuinely good — the FTP-Dreiklang, the load-verdict table, the zone-accent bars. But the interface repeatedly gets in its own way: a decorative photo undermines the exact page you're using to plan today's ride, the headline "what should I do today" advice can contradict itself, and the typography system the DESIGN.md describes isn't actually reaching the browser. The single biggest opportunity: strip the showroom layer (photo, 3D tilt, unloaded fonts) and let the already-strong data honesty carry the page.

## What's Working

1. **FTP-Dreiklang block (`/analysis`)** — GEMESSEN / GESCHÄTZT / ZIEL shown as three separate labelled rows with source + date, refusing to conflate measured vs. estimated vs. goal. That intellectual honesty is the product's real voice.
2. **Load table's EINORDNUNG column** — every week gets a verdict plus reason ("Ramp +7,5 CTL/Woche — leicht über dem Korridor, beobachten"), turning numbers into a decision instead of leaving the user to interpret them.
3. **Per-type accent bars in the week grid** — a 3px colored edge per cell encodes session type across 91 cells with almost no added visual weight. This is the one place the zone-color-as-meaning system is actually working as designed.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Full-bleed photo wrecks legibility of the page you use to plan training**
- **Why it matters:** `/planning` — the most actively-worked, most-used surface right now — has calendar rows and 10px date labels sitting directly over bright regions of a fixed background photo, worst around KW25–KW30. It's scroll-position-dependent, so it's unpredictable, not a one-off.
- **Fix:** Confine the photo to the Hero's first viewport only; put a near-opaque scrim/plate behind content on `/planning`, `/analysis`, `/log` instead of the current near-transparent cell fill.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish` (or `/impeccable quieter` if the photo turns out to be an intentional brand choice worth keeping, just toned down)

**[P1] The typography system isn't shipping**
- **Why it matters:** Both assessments hit this independently from different angles: Assessment A found `app/index.html` has no font link/`@font-face` for Sora/IBM Plex Mono/Inter, so every "display number" is a system fallback; the detector confirmed `overused-font: Inter` on all 4 pages plus dozens of `undersized-ui-text`/`tiny-text` hits (9.9–11.2px) concentrated in labels and table headers. The documented design identity and the shipped product have diverged.
- **Fix:** Self-host and preload the three woff2 fonts; raise the smallest label sizes off the 9.9–10.9px floor (project's own DESIGN.md reserves that range for secondary meta only, not primary labels like table headers and day-of-week tags).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

**[P1] Day cells tell screen readers they're disabled, but they're the only way to see session detail**
- **Why it matters:** All 73 card cells in the week grid carry `aria-disabled="true"` (inherited from the drag library) while still being clickable/keyboard-openable, and the status glyph (✓/!/×) is `aria-hidden`. A keyboard or screen-reader user is told the control is unavailable and can't perceive status at all — and the interval structure for a session is *only* reachable through that same control.
- **Fix:** Only spread the drag-disabled attributes when dragging is actually enabled; set your own `role="button" tabIndex={0}` otherwise; replace the hidden glyph with a visually-hidden status label.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` (accessibility pass), then `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The Hero's top advice can contradict itself and prints raw ISO dates**
- **Why it matters:** `briefing.js` has no rest-day branch, so the live output can read "Nächste Einheit ('Ruhetag', 2026-08-19) entschärfen oder verschieben — Umfang ist okay" right next to "Heute: Ruhetag." This is the single most prominent line of advice on the page and it can be nonsense; the raw ISO date also breaks the project's own DD.MM convention.
- **Fix:** Route dates through `fmtDate()`; add a rest-day guard that swaps in a non-contradictory message.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] "Analyse" has no charts — all visualizations live under "Explorer" instead**
- **Why it matters:** `features/analysis/` imports zero chart components; all 20+ live in `ExplorerPage.tsx`/`HeroPage.tsx`/`LogbookPage.tsx`. A coach opening "Analyse" to check whether CTL is ramping finds only tables and bar strips, with no indication the actual trend chart is one tab over.
- **Fix:** Either merge Explorer's PMC/FTP-forecast charts into `/analysis` and drop the Explorer route, or rename both honestly ("Analyse" → "Kennzahlen", "Explorer" → "Verläufe") and cross-link.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`, then `/impeccable clarify` for the renaming/cross-linking

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):** Zero `:focus`/`:hover` rules across the loaded stylesheets — all interactive Hero elements are inline-styled, so pseudo-class states are structurally impossible; focus is whatever the browser draws by default over a translucent tile on a photo background. "Check-in offen" — the daily primary action — is a button styled with `background:none; border:none; padding:0`, indistinguishable from a status label. The week grid's 73 `aria-disabled` cells (see P1 above) compound this. `/log`'s table header isn't sticky over 92 rows.

**Alex (power user):** Tabbing from the Planungstab header to the table costs 73 stops with no skip link. Interval structure text truncates at a fixed 104px cell width, forcing a click per cell to read it. The Fahrtenbuch's EINHEIT and TYP columns frequently disagree on the same row with no header explaining which is the planned card vs. the inferred type. No saved filters, no shortcuts, no column customization on a table used daily.

## Minor Observations

- Hero content is a cursor-tracked 3D tilt plate whose base rotation alone produces a permanent horizontal scrollbar (measured ~1442px content in a 1425px column) and clips the `<h1>` on mobile at 390px.
- "0 TSS" shows on 12 of 13 week rows (sums a field that's usually null) while the Hero reports real TSS for the same week elsewhere — a visible internal contradiction.
- SOLL/IST and COMPLIANCE table columns are 100% empty dashes; "Plantreue 28 Tage: –"; one instance of "1 ml/Tag Ø" hydration with a "Dehydration" warning attached.
- Three different "active" pill treatments across nav / athlete-toggle / Explorer time-range, none matching the SS-orange fill AGENTS.md specifies for active tabs.
- Long line-lengths flagged by the detector on `/analysis` (up to ~258 chars/line) — worth a max-width check on prose blocks.
- 13 em-dashes in `/analysis` body copy — mild but worth a copy pass.
- Section headers use arbitrary emoji (🔵📊🫀⚡♻📅) mapping to nothing in the zone-color system.
- Console warning on every load: "Multiple GoTrueClient instances detected."
- `radial-spotlight-glow` / `dark-glow` / `hero-eyebrow-chip` / `kicker-above-heading` detector hits on the Hero line up with Assessment A's "showroom" critique of the photo+tilt treatment — worth a look together, not one-off nitpicks.
